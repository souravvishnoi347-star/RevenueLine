FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Propnexaa replace our existing CRM? +

No. Propnexaa integrates with your existing CRM — PropSpace, HubSpot, Zoho, Bitrix24, or Salesforce. We automate data entry, workflows, and follow-ups without requiring you to migrate systems.

Is this compliant with UAE data laws? +

Yes. Data processing strictly adheres to the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL). Communication workflows respect RERA and Trakheesi advertising guidelines.

Does automation replace human sales agents? +

No. Automation handles the initial response, data gathering, and qualification. Once intent is established, the prospect is handed off to a human broker for negotiation and closing.
